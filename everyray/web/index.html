Municipal budget

Direct action ranks the town's own buildings (schools, city hall, health centre…) by payback and funds their PV outright. Subsidies rank zones of private buildings (residential/commercial/industrial) by payback and cover 30% of their PV+battery cost, leaving owners to fund the rest and keep the savings.

This space is reserved for an assistant that helps a municipality go from model to action: finding installer and financing partners, walking through the assumptions behind a specific number, or explaining why a given zone or budget split was recommended. Not built in this demo — just marking where it belongs.

Buildings, sun and weather are real open data (OpenStreetMap, PVGIS). Household consumption is simulated (typical Spanish load profiles) — Catarroja's own smart-meter data would replace it in a pilot. Costs and tariffs are indicative assumptions — see the Assumptions drawer.
